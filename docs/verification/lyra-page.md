## Lyra

**Meeting notes that stay on your Mac.**

Lyra records your calls and in-person meetings, then turns them into notes you own: a clean transcript,
who said what, and expanded notes built from the points you typed.

- **Coming up:** connect your Google or Microsoft calendar to see the week's meetings and join each call
  in one click.
- **Private by design:** recordings, transcripts and voice fingerprints never leave your Mac. Speech is
  turned into text on your Mac.
- **Your notes, your files:** every meeting is a plain Markdown note in the folder you choose, such as an
  Obsidian vault.
- **Knows who's talking:** Lyra learns voices, and on video calls it reads whose tile is lit.

Lyra reads your calendar only to list your upcoming meetings. It never changes your calendar, and
your events are never stored or sent anywhere. [Privacy policy](/lyra/privacy)
