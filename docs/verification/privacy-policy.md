# Lyra privacy policy

*Draft for your own domain/lyra/privacy. Last updated 28 October 2026.*

Lyra is a meeting recorder and notes app for Mac, made by Aidan Mascaro in Perth, Western Australia.
Everything Lyra keeps stays on your Mac. Lyra has no server and no analytics, so I never receive your calendar,
recordings, notes or anything else.

## Your calendar (Google and Microsoft)

When you connect a calendar, Lyra asks Google or Microsoft for permission to **read** your events. It can never
change, add or delete them.

- **What Lyra reads:** your events from today to 7 days ahead. For each one it uses the title, the start and end
  times, whether it's all-day, how many other people are invited, whether you declined, and the video-call link.
- **Why:** to list your upcoming meetings on Lyra's home page and give each one a Join button that opens the call.
- **Where it's kept:** only in Lyra's memory while it's open. Lyra fetches the list again every few minutes and
  never saves your events to disk.
- **When the title is saved:** if you press Join and Lyra then records the meeting, the event's title becomes that
  note's title. It's saved with the note on your Mac.
- **Your sign-in:** Lyra keeps the sign-in from Google or Microsoft in a file on your Mac that only your user
  account can open. It reads your email address once, to label the account in Lyra's menu.

## Recordings, transcripts and notes

- Lyra records only after you click Allow, or after you press Join on a meeting in Lyra.
- Audio and transcripts are saved in your Documents folder. Lyra turns speech into text on your Mac.
- Voice fingerprints, which Lyra uses to recognise who's speaking, are kept on your Mac and never leave it.
- If you allow it, Lyra reads the call window to see whose video tile is lit, and saves shared slides when you
  switch that on. This also happens entirely on your Mac.

## Claude

To write the expanded notes and suggest a title and tags, Lyra sends the transcript, your typed notes and the
meeting's title to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. It goes through Claude Code, already signed in on your Mac,
under your own Claude account and your own agreement with Anthropic. If you started the recording with Join,
that title came from your calendar. Nothing else from your calendar is sent to Claude or anyone else.

## Removing your data

- **Calendar:** in Lyra, open the **…** menu next to "Coming up" and choose **Remove from Lyra**. This deletes
  the saved sign-in. You can also remove Lyra's access at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) or
  [account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access](https://account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access).
- **Recordings and notes:** they're ordinary files on your Mac. Delete them whenever you like.

## Google user data

Lyra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. Lyra doesn't sell Google data, doesn't use it for advertising, and
doesn't use it to develop, improve or train AI models.
