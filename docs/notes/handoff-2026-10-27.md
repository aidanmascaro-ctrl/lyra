`allWritten` accept a grey heading's hash at any level it was ever written. 378 core tests green; the four real
notes were brought over at 21:10 and Obsidian re-rendered them (folded "Full transcript" box confirmed by
screenshot). Not yet checked by Aidan: whether `###` topic headings in Minimal (semibold, body size, a notch
